# ECLIPSE — Art Style Bible v1
*Phase 0 deliverable | GDD refs: 2.2 (the Radiance), 3.2–3.4 (planet identities, production rules), 1.7 (signature moments) | Skill owner: World Builder*

---

## 1. The One Idea: Light as Power

All ECLIPSE art direction flows from a single duality (GDD 2.2): **the Dominion owns the light; the rebellion is the shadow that chooses to block it.**

- **Dominion visual language:** white-gold, symmetrical, monumental, *lit* — sun-mirrors, radiant plazas, the sun sigil. Identical architecture on every planet (fiction: standardization as oppression; production: one shared kit — GDD 3.4.1).
- **Rebellion visual language:** warm, improvised, asymmetrical, *sheltered* — sodium-orange work light, welded plate, hand-painted eclipse sigils. Materials are always visibly repurposed (a rebel wall is a door from somewhere else).
- **The reading rule:** in any shot, the viewer must be able to tell whose space they are in within one second, from color temperature and geometry alone.

## 2. Global Principles

1. **One-screenshot identity (GDD 3.2):** each planet readable from a single frame — enforced via the palette table below. No two planets share a dominant hue.
2. **Grounded tone (GDD 1.4.5):** *The Expanse* meets *Andor*. No glow-for-glow's-sake, no space fantasy. Every material has a job; wear patterns tell labor stories.
3. **State layers (GDD 3.4.5):** every region dresses three states — **Occupied** (Dominion light dominates, propaganda clean), **Contested** (broken light, scorch, barricades), **Liberated** (warm mixed light, sigils, civilian life returns). Implemented as Data Layers; the war's progress must be visible without UI.
4. **Human scale contrast (Pillar 1):** Dominion structures are framed to dwarf people; rebel spaces are framed at shoulder height. The camera language *is* the climb.
5. **Readability before richness:** silhouette and value contrast beat detail density. Enemy archetypes must read by silhouette (GDD 9.3); planet palettes reserve one accent color for gameplay-critical elements.

## 4. UI Art Direction

Diegetic-leaning, restrained (GDD 8.8: no HUD soup). Strategy/base layers use a "resistance command table" material language (projected light on scavenged surfaces) that gains Dominion-grade crispness as the player's tech rises — UI itself follows the climb. Colorblind-safe accent system from day one.

## 5. Concept Piece Briefs (one per planet — Phase 0 checklist item)

> Execution requires an artist (or supervised image pipeline) — **open Phase 0 item**; the briefs below are the commission-ready half of the deliverable. Format: one key image, 16:9, mood + story in a single frame, palette locked to §3.

1. **Kessara — "The Ration Line":** Underworks street at shift change; amber smog, crane forests silhouetted above; a Dominion checkpoint's white-gold light slicing a queue of workers. One chalk eclipse sigil, half-washed away. *(Sells: oppression, verticality, the two light languages.)*
2. **Tarsis — "Hull-Right":** dawn over a dune sea; a kilometer-long megaship carcass as horizon; scavenger convoy dwarfed in its shadow; storm-violet lightning on the horizon. *(Sells: scale, salvage culture.)*
3. **Krad-9 — "Shared Air":** an ore cathedral interior; hard white surface light stabbing down a shaft onto a pit-clan crew at a pressure door; oxygen-blue seals glowing. *(Sells: enclosure, brotherhood, breach danger.)*
4. **Vel'Naar — "The Long Walk":** blizzard clearing to reveal a black brutalist prison fortress sunk in an ice shelf under aurora; a rope-line of escapees crossing the foreground. *(Sells: cold, hope at cost.)*
5. **The Shroud — "Nym Docks":** neon dockyard promenade on the domed moon; the gas giant's amber storm face filling half the sky; a corvette (the *Loyal Ghost*) under crane light. *(Sells: smuggler romance, first fleet fantasy.)*
6. **Meridia — "Jade and Gold":** float-city trade quarter in rain; pearl platforms, gold trade-house banners, Bursary patrol boat below; a memorial coast barely visible in the haze. *(Sells: wealth, politics, buried grief.)*
7. **Sylvaris — "The Green Cathedral":** mid-trunk city on a 300 m tree; god-rays; a burned trunk-city scar visible in the distance, kept as memorial space, not spectacle (GDD 3.3.7). *(Sells: verticality, the Reprisals' weight.)*
8. **Elystra — "The Garden":** golden wheat to the horizon, white arcology gleaming, Radiant Guard ceremonial patrol on a spotless road — unsettling perfection, no visible dirt anywhere. *(Sells: comfort as control.)*
9. **Vorn — "The Anvil":** ash storm parting over the Vorn Yards; colossal ship skeletons in gantries lit by ember fissures and arc-weld blue; conscript labor columns in the foreground. *(Sells: the enemy's industrial heart.)*
10. **Aurelion — "Eclipse":** the poster image (GDD 1.7 Moment 7): the rebel fleet physically eclipsing the sun-mirror array above the Radiant Spire; white-gold monumentalism falling into deep blue shadow; city lights igniting below as districts rise. *(Sells: the title, the whole game.)*
